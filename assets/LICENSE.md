# Asset credits

All models (`models/*.gmd`) and sounds (`sounds/*.ogg`) in this folder come from
Kenney's starter kits and are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).

- Models and sounds: Kenney — https://kenney.nl (via https://github.com/KenneyNL)
  - Starter-Kit-Racing, Starter-Kit-FPS, Starter-Kit-City-Builder,
    Starter-Kit-3D-Platformer, Starter-Kit-Basic-Scene ("Mini Arena" pack,
    additional credit Tony Schär)
- `sounds/skid.ogg`: Landeplage (CC0)

The models were converted from GLB with `tools/convert_models.py` (textures are
baked into vertex colours). Run `tools/fetch_assets.sh` to regenerate everything.
